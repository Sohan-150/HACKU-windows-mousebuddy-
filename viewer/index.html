What should it do?

Type, or hold the talk keys (Ctrl+Win) and talk. Point the mouse at anything and ask "what is this?", "where is save?" or "circle the zebra". Typed questions about the screen ("what am I looking at?") use the window you were in.

It works in its own browser window or a desktop app, behind your windows, or directly on files in your user folder. Anything that looks irreversible (send, pay, submit, save, any file change) waits for your approval. It never deletes files, never overwrites them, and never types passwords or card numbers.
